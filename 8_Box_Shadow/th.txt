Box-Shadow
==========
Providing shadows to HTML elements

Soft Shadows 
Hard Shadows
Glowing Effects
3D Effects 

box-shadow : horizontal-offset vertical-offset blur-radius spread-radius color;
box-shadow : 5px 5px 10px 10px gray


horizontal-offset : move shadow left(-) or right(+)
vertical-offset : move shadow up(-) or down(+)
blur-radius : controls softness of shadow
spread-radius : increase or decrease the size of shadow
color : shadow color
